# Covariate Sensitivity Pipeline 
### AML-Exome-Burden-Analysis-Top-Genes-PRS.ipynb
- This script generates a rare variant polygenic risk score file for the top three significant genes identified in the SAIGE-GENE+ set-based analysis. The resulting file contains one column per gene, representing the rare-variant polygenic risk score burden for that gene for each individual. Each predicted deleterious rare variant (missense or loss-of-function; MAF < 1%) present within a gene for a given individual is assigned a weight of 1. The gene-level score is then computed as the sum of all predicted deleterious rare variants present within that gene for each individual.

### AML-Top-Genes-Sensitivity-Analysis.ipynb
- This script performs logistic regression and Cox regression using the rare variant polygenic risk scores for the top genes. Covariates for the logistic regression include age, sex, ten principal components, exome burden, and either the electronic health record index date or the observation period. Covariates for the Cox regression—modeling time from birth or time from the first electronic health record—include sex, ten principal components, exome burden, year of birth or year of first electronic health record, and optionally the electronic health record index date.

### AML-Top-Genes-With-Covariates-Sensitivity-Analysis.ipynb
- This script performs logistic regression and Cox regression using the rare variant polygenic risk scores for the top genes. Covariates for the logistic regression include age, sex, ten principal components, exome burden, and either the electronic health record index date or the observation period. Covariates for the Cox regression—modeling time from birth or time from the first electronic health record—include sex, ten principal components, exome burden, year of birth or year of first electronic health record, and optionally the electronic health record index date. For this analysis, additional covariates were included in the model to assess whether their inclusion would improve the significance of the top genes. In particular, both smoking status and prior exposure to alkylating agents before the first electronic health record corresponding to acute myeloid leukemia were included as covariates.

Covariates for the Cox regression—modeling time from birth or time from the first electronic health record—include sex, ten principal components, exome burden, year of birth or year of first electronic health record, and either the deprivation index or the components of the deprivation index. For this analysis, additional covariates were included in the model to assess whether their inclusion would improve the significance of the top genes.
